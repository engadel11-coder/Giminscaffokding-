Cuplock Node Joint Inspection (0.5m Spacing)

Joint Mechanism

The Cuplock node features a unique circular locking principle:

  1. Fixed Bottom Cup: Welded to the standard at 0.5m intervals.
  2. Ledger Blades: Forged ends of up to 4 horizontal members slot into the bottom cup.
  3. Top Sliding Cup: Dropped down and rotated by hammer strike to securely lock all four components into a rigid node.
Engineering Advantage: Zero loose fittings or bolts; rigid joint resistance reduces sway bracing requirements.

Cuplock System Engineering Reference

Design Standards Compliance

Designed in accordance with EN 12810-1, BS 1139 Part 3, and ANSI A10.8 safety standards for system scaffolds.

1. Standard Tube Specifications

High-yield steel tube: Outer Diameter 48.3 mm x 3.2 mm wall thickness (Grade S355 / Grade Q345 galvanised steel).

2. Permissible Standard Leg Loads

  • Unbraced Lift Height 2.0m: 57.0 kN allowable leg axial load.
  • Unbraced Lift Height 1.5m: 68.5 kN allowable leg axial load.
  • Base Jack Maximum Adjustment: 600 mm (Minimum 150mm insertion inside tube).

3. Working Platform Load Duties

  • Light Duty (1.5 kN/m²): Inspection, painting, and light hand tools.
  • Medium Duty (2.0 kN/m²): General building work, plastering, lightweight brickwork.
  • Heavy Duty (3.0 kN/m²): Heavy masonry, stone dressing, or material storage on platform.
